Our story

Local riders, local roads

Rev Cycling started in Mirissa with three friends, a few bikes and a love for the quiet roads behind the beach. Today we run guided tours for riders of every level.


We keep groups small, use well-maintained hybrid and road bikes, and work with village families, cafés and tea growers so the money you spend stays in the community.

  • Groups of 8 riders or fewer
  • First-aid trained, English-speaking guides
  • Support vehicle on longer routes

Safety and care

Certified, trained and ready

Rev Cycling is run by certified cycling professionals. Every guide is trained to lead a group, read the road and look after your comfort from the first pedal to the last.

  • Certified cycling professionals as lead guides
  • First-aid training and a route briefing before every ride
  • Bikes checked and fitted to each rider every morning
  • Support vehicle and local emergency contacts on every route
